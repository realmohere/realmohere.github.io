Meetout

An app for meeting new people around Oss through small public hangouts: post a plan, others ask to join, the group chats and meets. References after each meetup build trust.

My role
Product owner: defined the product, its rules and safety features, and built it with AI coding tools

Approach

A mobile-first web app with an Android version. Someone posts a hangout at a public spot, others ask to join, and the host accepts or declines. The group chats live, and after meeting up people leave references with descriptive tags. Safety is built in: an 18+ check, public meeting spots, and negative tags that only the person concerned can see. The launch version runs on Firebase with phone sign-in and data stored in the Netherlands.
