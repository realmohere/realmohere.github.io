Hydrogen-generation prototype

A steel-plate hydrogen-generation system, taken from first concept through construction to practical tests of its gas output.

My role
Designed, built and tested it myself
Outcome
A working prototype, tested for gas output.
  • Mechanical design
  • Prototyping
  • Testing

The problem

A hands-on side project: could a simple design built around steel plates generate hydrogen gas, and what does it take to get from a concept on paper to a device that actually works?

Approach

I worked from concept to construction: designing a system around steel plates, building it, and then testing in practice how much gas it produced.

My contribution

All of it: the design, the build and the testing.

Outcome

A working prototype that produced gas in practical tests.
