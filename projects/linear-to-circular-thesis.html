04 · M.A. thesis

From Linear to Circular

How policy, institutions, incentives and cooperation shape the move from linear to circular production in post-industrial Duisburg, compared with Amsterdam and Singapore.

My role
Author, written with Duisburg Business & Innovation GmbH
Outcome
Completed as part of the M.A. in Philosophy, Politics & Economics.
  • Circular economy
  • Policy analysis
  • Economic resilience

The problem

Full title: From Linear to Circular: Policy Pathways for Economic Resilience and Sustainability in Duisburg's Post-Industrial Transition. The question was what shapes the shift from linear to circular production in a post-industrial city.

Approach

A policy and institutional analysis of Duisburg, looking at policy, institutions, incentives and cooperation, with Amsterdam and Singapore as comparative cases. Written alongside my work as a project assistant at Duisburg Business & Innovation GmbH.

My contribution

Sole author.

Outcome

Part of my completed M.A. in Philosophy, Politics & Economics at Witten/Herdecke University.
