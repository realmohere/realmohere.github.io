Dogout

Dog play dates in Noord-Brabant, starting in Oss. Owners make a profile for their dog, get matched with dogs of similar energy, size and play style, and plan open or private play dates.

My role
Product owner, adapting the Meetout codebase to a new audience
Outcome
In development, not public yet.
  • Product design
  • React
  • Firebase
  • Dutch and English

The problem

Finding good play partners for a dog is hit and miss. Dogs differ a lot in energy, size and play style, and a poor match makes a play date stressful for dogs and owners.

Approach

Built from a copy of Meetout: a React and TypeScript web app in Dutch and English on Firebase, with an Android version. Matching uses each dog's energy, size and play style, and a Hangout view shows which dogs are out nearby right now.

My contribution

I defined the product and adapted the Meetout codebase for it, working with AI coding tools.

Outcome

In development and not public yet.
