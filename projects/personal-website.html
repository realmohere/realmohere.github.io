The problem

I wanted one place that explains my mix of engineering, economics and policy better than a CV can, and that people and search engines can actually find.

Approach

A single static page without a framework, hosted on GitHub Pages under my own domain. Structured data (JSON-LD), a sitemap and an llms.txt file help search engines and AI assistants understand it. GoatCounter counts visits without cookies.

My contribution

Design, writing and build, working with AI coding tools.

Outcome

Live at momohammadi.nl, verified in Google Search Console and imported into Bing Webmaster Tools.
