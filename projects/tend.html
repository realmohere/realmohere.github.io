Tend

A no-shame life game for adults with ADHD, informed by CBT techniques: small real-life steps help your avatar grow, and missing a day never costs you anything.

My role
Product owner, working with a team of AI role agents
Outcome
A playable prototype for playtesting.
  • Game design
  • Behaviour change
  • JavaScript
  • AI agents

The problem

Many habit apps punish a missed day with a lost streak. For adults with ADHD that often turns a tool meant to help into one more source of shame.

Approach

A playtest prototype that runs in the browser with no accounts. The game rules are written as tested functions. The project is run like a small company, with AI agents in roles from product and design to privacy, marketing and finance, each following written rules. Tend is a self-help game, not a treatment.

My contribution

I set the direction and decisions as product owner and coordinate the AI role agents.

Outcome

A playable browser prototype for playtesting. It has not been released publicly.
