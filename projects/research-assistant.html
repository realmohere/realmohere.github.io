02 · Research tool

GPT-based research assistant

An assistant that finds start-ups and organises their contact details by country and city, to speed up market and partner research.

My role
Designed and built it
Outcome
A working tool that groups start-ups and contacts by country and city.
  • AI tools
  • Market research
  • Automation

The problem

Finding start-ups in a market and collecting their contact details by hand is slow, and the results end up scattered and hard to use for outreach.

Approach

A GPT-based workflow that identifies start-ups and structures their contact information by country and city, so the results can be used directly for outreach and partner research.

My contribution

I designed the workflow and built the assistant.

Outcome

A working assistant that returns start-ups with their contact details grouped by country and city, ready for outreach and partner research.
